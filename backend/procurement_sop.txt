# SOP-14: Emergency Procurement and Shortage Resolution

## 1. Scope
This standard operating procedure covers immediate actions to be taken by the Inventory and Procurement teams when an unexpected shortage of raw materials is detected prior to a scheduled production run.

## 2. Authorized Actions for Aluminum Alloy 6061
If a shortage of Aluminum Alloy 6061 is detected and threatens a target deadline:
- For shortages greater than 50 units: Production must be paused or rescheduled. Supervisor approval is required.
- For shortages of 50 units or fewer (<= 50): Expedited shipping from Supplier B is pre-authorized. Proceed with the order to meet the deadline.

## 3. Other Materials
Standard 2-week lead times apply. No expedited shipping authorized without VP approval.
